What the quote form collects

When you send the quote request form, we receive the fields you fill in: your name, company, email address, the property address, the type of work you need, and your description of the scope and target date.

We ask for these only so we can answer your request. You choose what to type; fields such as the property address are not verified by the site.

Where it goes

Each submission is written to a Google Sheet that our team uses to follow up on quote requests. Google processes that data as our service provider.

The site does not send form data to advertising networks.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not set cookies.

We measure traffic with anonymous analytics. It does not use identifiers, does not follow you across other websites, and is not tied to the information you send in the form.

Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as the time of a request, as any web host does.

Deleting your information

To have a quote request removed, write to the contact email listed on this page and tell us which request is yours (the email address you used is enough). We may need to confirm that the request comes from the person who submitted it. Once confirmed, we will delete the entry from the sheet within 30 days.

Copies may persist for a time in the sheet's version history, in backups and in notification emails about the submission, and we cannot promise when or whether they disappear from those systems.

Changes

If what the site does with information changes, this page will change with it.
